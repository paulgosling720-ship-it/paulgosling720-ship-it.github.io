Life Bot privacy policy

Life Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner, Paul Gosling. Only the owner's own Google accounts are connected to it.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: reads events to send reminders and summaries, and adds events the owner asks for.
  • Gmail (read-only): reads the sender, subject and preview of recent inbox messages to flag important ones. It never sends, deletes or changes email.

Where data goes

Information is shown only to the owner, in his private Discord messages. Sign-in tokens and a small reminder database are stored on the owner's own server. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising, and no data is passed to anyone else.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
